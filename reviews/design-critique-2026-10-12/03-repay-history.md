# 03 Repayments, history (Activity), expense detail, corrections and voids

Evidence level: source and design frames only. Nothing was built or run (no Xcode in this container). Every finding says "verified in code" (I read the lines), "inferred" (follows from code but needs a Simulator look) or "design only".

Paths below are relative to `ios/NumaShared/NumaShared/` unless stated. Frames are `design/interface-v2` canvas PNGs.

## Verdict

The money path is better than the screens around it. Recording a repayment in a two-group household is already two taps with the exact debt prefilled (Home "Record a repayment", then "Mark as paid"; `AppModel.swift:4629-4641`, verified in code). Everything that should make history trustworthy is where it falls apart. Corrections and voids are folded into the original row instead of appearing as the visible events that frames 19 and 20 promise, and the "Corrections" filter finds none of them. Repayment rows carry a baked, viewer-relative sentence ("Sam paid your household") that is wrong on every phone but the recorder's. Five different nouns (repayment, settlement, transfer, payment, reversal) describe one thing. Fixing a wrong repayment takes about nine taps and three events. Overpayment is one line of 15pt text instead of the designed warning. Activity is a hard-cut sheet with no date grouping, no amount search and filters that never reset. The core contract is sound (append-only, flag then linked reversal); the presentation layer does not yet tell that story.

Tap counts (verified in code):

| Task | Taps today | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Full repayment, two groups | 2 | 2 (keep) |
| Partial repayment (e.g. €30 of €84) | 5 (Record, Clear, 3, 0, Mark) | 3 |
| Correct an expense amount | about 7 (row, scroll, Edit, 4 backspaces or Clear plus digits, Save) | 4 |
| Void an expense | 4 plus typing a reason (row, scroll, Void, type, Confirm) | 3, no typing |
| Fix a wrong repayment | about 9 across 3 events | 4 |

## Findings

### 1. Corrections and voids are not visible events in the list; the "Corrections" filter finds nothing
- Severity: S1 broken/trust
- Evidence: `LedgerRepository.swift:1554-1658` builds exactly one `ExpenseRecord` per record; edits live in `revisionHistory`, voids in `voided`. `Models.swift:413-429` `badge` returns `nil` for an edited `.expense`, so a corrected expense looks identical to an untouched one. `Components.swift:475` only greys the title of a voided row and `:507` leaves the amount unstruck. `Models.swift:189-192` `ActivityKindFilter.corrections` only matches `.expenseRevision/.voidAttempt/.restoreAttempt/.settlementFlagAttempt/.failedAttempt`. Those kinds are only created for events that failed delivery (`LedgerRepository.swift:1889-1951`). Frames 19 and 20 show "Dinner corrected · You · €96.00 → €90.00 · −€6.00", "Dinner voided · You · original retained", an "Original entry · corrected/voided" row, and a struck-through amount.
- Why it matters: "append-only, visible corrections" is a hard product contract, and today a flatmate who sees €90 where they remembered €96 has no signal in the list that anything changed, who changed it, or why. The only trace is the detail sheet's "REVISION HISTORY" table (`SheetViews.swift:586-625`) for edits and a banner for voids.
- Fix: derive presentation items, not new ledger events. For each record emit its original row (badge "Corrected" or "Voided", amount struck when voided, subtitle "Original entry · corrected") plus one story row per revision after the first and per void/restore. Story rows read "Sam changed Dinner €96 to €90" with the delta on the right ("−€6.00") and the voided case "Sam voided Dinner · 'Added twice'". Make `.corrections` match any record with `revisionHistory.count > 1` or `voided`. Void actor and time are not on `ExpenseRecord` (only `voidReason`). The repository already holds `eventByID` at `:1722`, so add `voidedBy`/`voidedAt` from the latest `recordVoided` event. Rename the filter "Changes".
- Effort: M. Confidence: verified in code.

### 2. Repayment titles are a baked, viewer-relative sentence stored as authored text
- Severity: S1 broken/trust
- Evidence: `AppModel.swift:4896-4911` stores `title = "<from name> paid <to name>"`, substituting the literal string "your household" when the receiver is the recorder's own group. `LedgerRepository.swift:1682` shows `record.content.title` verbatim, and `Components.swift:475` and `SheetViews.swift:228` render it as the headline. `docs/PERSISTENCE.md:8` says `title` is an erasable authored-text sidecar that hydrates as `[redacted]` once deleted.
- Why it matters: on Sam's phone a repayment Leo recorded reads "Sam paid your household", which looks like Sam paid himself. Names are frozen at write time, so a renamed group or edited person shows stale text. After the recorder deletes their account every repayment they entered becomes a row titled "[redacted]". Reversals inherit it ("Reversal of Sam paid your household", `:4905`).
- Fix: the repayment's meaning is already structured (`payer`, `to`, amount). Store a constant title ("Repayment"; it satisfies the non-blank rule) and render the sentence at display time from live group names: "Sam paid Alex + Jo back". Do the same for reversals ("Correction of Sam's repayment"). Legacy rows get the same treatment because the view ignores the stored title for `.settlement`.
- Effort: S to M. Confidence: verified in code; the `[redacted]` behaviour is inferred from `docs/PERSISTENCE.md`.

### 3. Fixing a wrong repayment is a nine-tap, three-event, three-vocabulary maze
- Severity: S1 broken/trust. Challenges a decision (the UX of it, not the immutability).
- Evidence: `SheetViews.swift:361-365` ("Settlements are immutable — flag it if something is wrong; it stays visible while you sort it out."), `:423-453` ("Flag as incorrect", "Record reversal", "Fully reversed by linked records."), `:991-996` ("Reverse flagged settlement"), `:325-333` ("Linked reversals"). `AppModel.swift:4603-4627` flags immediately with no explanation. `:4731-4748` opens the reversal. `Reducer.swift:745` requires the flag first, and `DECISIONS.md:73` records flag then linked reversal as the v1 correction path.
- Why it matters: the most likely money mistake in the app is a typo on the repayment keypad (€500 for €50). Once the 6-second undo has passed, the flatmate must discover "Flag as incorrect" (red, no consequence copy), then "Record reversal", re-enter or confirm an amount in a sheet titled "Reverse flagged settlement", then go back to Home and record the repayment again. A flag has no financial effect and nobody is notified, so that step is pure ceremony.
- Fix: keep the contract (original stays, linked opposite-direction record) but hide the mechanism behind one "Correct this repayment" action: a sheet with two plain choices ("The amount was wrong" with a keypad prefilled with the original, and "This didn't happen" which reverses in full), a one-line preview ("You + Sam owe Alex + Jo €34 → €84"), and one confirm. The app appends flag, reversal and optional replacement in one held batch with a single Undo, and the list shows one story row ("Sam corrected a repayment €500 to €50"). Also offer flag with a reason (`SettlementFlaggedPayload.reason` exists, `appendFlag` never sets it, `LedgerRepository.swift:867-880`). Delete the words flag, reversal, linked and immutable from the UI.
- Effort: L (multi-event batch plus grouped undo). Confidence: verified in code.

### 4. Overpayment is one small line, not the designed moment, and the helper code is dead
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: frame 36 gives overpayment its own screen ("A little more than you owe.", amber box "This is €16.00 over your balance. If €100 really changed hands, record it…", "Change amount"). Native only changes the preview line at `AppModel.swift:4825-4840` ("Overpayment reverses who owes whom. After this, Alex + Jo owe You + Sam €16.00. Only record money already sent elsewhere.") rendered at `SheetViews.swift:929-930` as `.subheadline.weight(.semibold)`. `useOverpay()` and `overpaySuggestion` (`AppModel.swift:4756-4777`) are referenced by no view.
- Why it matters: a stray extra digit flips the whole debt with one tap and a line that is easy to read as boilerplate. The wording "reverses who owes whom" is also the scary version of a normal situation (rounding up, paying ahead).
- Fix: when the amount exceeds the debt, show an inline notice block (icon plus tint, not red) above the CTA. Copy: "That's €16.00 more than you owe. Alex + Jo would then owe You + Sam €16.00." Add a one-tap "Use €84.00" chip. Make the CTA read "Record €100.00 repaid" and require a second confirm tap only in this state; the exact-amount path stays one tap. For singleton households say "You'd be €16.00 ahead". Delete the dead `useOverpay` code or wire it to a "Round up" chip.
- Effort: S to M. Confidence: verified in code; design frame 36.

### 5. The prefilled amount and the keypad fight each other
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: `MoneyInput.swift:46-55`. A digit appends to the whole part when there is no fraction and is silently ignored when the fraction already has `exponent` digits. Settlement prefill stores no fraction for round debts (`AppModel.swift:4779-4786`), so tapping 5, 0 after a prefilled €84 gives €8450. Edit prefill always stores "00" (`:4468-4474`), so tapping 9, 0 to change €96.00 does nothing; the user needs four backspaces. Both sheets paper over this with a "Clear amount" button (`SheetViews.swift:675`, `:903`).
- Why it matters: partial repayments and amount corrections are the common non-default cases. The behaviour differs by whether the debt has cents, with dead keys in one case and a 100x error in the other.
- Fix: treat the prefilled value as selected. The first keypress or backspace replaces it (calculator and Apple Wallet behaviour), shown with a subtle highlighted amount. Add chips under the amount: "Full €84.00", "Half €42.00". Partial repayment drops from 5 taps to about 3.
- Effort: S. Confidence: verified in code.

### 6. The repayment sheet's primary button is probably below the fold and says the wrong thing
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: `SettleAmountSheet` (`SheetViews.swift:851-998`) is a `ScrollView` holding header, parties, a 34pt amount, currency line, Clear, Exact, preview text, guidance, a 3-line note, a full keypad, then "Mark as paid", "Record another transfer" and "Not yet". Adding the paddings gives about 865pt of content against roughly 815pt of `.large` sheet on a 6.3-inch phone, so the CTA scrolls off before the user touches anything; smaller phones and larger text are worse. Design frames 35 to 39 pin the CTA at the bottom ("Record €50.00 repaid") and the amount is 75pt display. "Mark as paid" (`:968`) reads as "mark the debt paid" even when the amount is partial. A back chevron, "Not yet" and swipe-down all do the same thing. "Record another transfer" shows up in a screen where the user just wants to confirm.
- Why it matters: this is the one screen where the user should never hunt for the confirm.
- Fix: pin the CTA with `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`, label it with the amount ("Record €84.00 repaid"), keep only one dismiss path (the chevron), and drop "Not yet", "Exact" (replaced by the chips from finding 5) and the "fixed household currency" line (`:901`). Show the keypad only once the user taps the amount, or place it under the CTA as a compact sheet. Move "Record another transfer" to a quiet text link on the plan list only. UI tests reference "Mark as paid" in `GoldenPathUITests.swift:72` and the `+HomeFidelity`/`+Settlements` extensions and need updating.
- Effort: M. Confidence: inferred from layout arithmetic (needs a Simulator check at default and AX sizes).

### 7. One thing, five names: repayment, settlement, transfer, payment, reversal
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: "Record a repayment" (Home `HomeView.swift:295`, sheet titles `SheetViews.swift:809`, `:997`), "Settlement" (`Models.swift:156`, `:360-372`, undo toast `AppModel.swift:4932` "Settlement €50 recorded"), "Settled" badge (`Models.swift:420`), "Settlements" filter (`:176`), "Settled so far" (`AppModel.swift:5144`), "Record another transfer"/"Record a transfer" (`SheetViews.swift:836`, `:993`), "Mark as paid" (`:968`), "Flag as incorrect"/"Reversal"/"immutable" (finding 3). The design says "Repayment", "Repayments", "Repayment recorded", "written down" (frames 18, 21, 35 to 39). Home's recent-activity row for a repayment uses the generic receipt icon plus "Settlement · Today · by Sam" instead of frame 21's swap-icon "Repayment recorded · You + Sam → Alex + Jo".
- Why it matters: "Settled" on a €50 repayment against an €84 debt says the debt is settled when €34 remains, and "transfer" is the bank word the product promises not to be.
- Fix: is "repayment" what people say? It is understood but stiff. Flatmates say "paid back" or "settle up", and "settle" is wrong for partial amounts. Use "paid back" in sentences ("Sam paid Alex + Jo back €50") and "Repayment" as the noun (button, filter, receipt). Ban "settlement", "settled", "transfer", "reversal", "flag" and "immutable" in UI strings. Remove the "Settled" badge (use none, or "Repayment"). Rename the filter "Repayments". Rename the CTA "Record €50.00 repaid". Home row copy follows frame 21.
- Effort: S. Confidence: verified in code; design frames 18, 21, 35 to 39.

### 8. Activity rows say "paid" twice, leak "units", and differ from Home
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: `Models.swift:379-394` builds the subtitle "%1$@ paid · %2$@ · split %3$lld units" (for example "Alex + Jo paid · Thu, 10 Sep · split 2 units"), and `Components.swift:490-497` adds a third line "Paid by Alex". The same record on Home (`HomeView.swift:464-468`) reads "Alex paid · yesterday". Activity amount uses `app.fmt(r.amountCents)` (`SheetViews.swift:170`, "€96") while Home and detail force cents ("€96.00", `HomeView.swift:428`). Home hard-codes `en_GB` for dates (`HomeView.swift:475`) while Activity uses the current locale. The design row is "Alex paid · added by Jo" with "€96.00" (frame 16).
- Why it matters: the history list is where people scan; the same expense shows a person on Home and a group in Activity, "units" is internal jargon, and amounts don't align because decimals appear and disappear.
- Fix: one shared row component for Home and Activity. Line 2 is "Alex paid · added by Jo" (person payer; "added by" only when the recorder differs). Drop the "split N units" text and the `Paid by` line. Always two decimals with `monospacedDigit` so amounts align. Under the total add a quiet "Your group's share €48.00" caption, since "my share vs total" is what people actually read. Drop the `en_GB` override.
- Effort: S. Confidence: verified in code.

### 9. No date grouping, no standing balance line, no year
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: `SheetViews.swift:153-185` is a flat `ForEach`; the date appears only inside the subtitle as "Today" or "Wed, 10 Sep" (`Models.swift:336-350`, no year, no "Yesterday"). The balance summary exists only when a group chip was tapped (`AppModel.swift:5098-5152`) and speaks ledger ("Share of expenses", "Paid for the pot", "Settled so far", `SheetViews.swift:144` "Balances cover the full pot history."). Frames 16 to 20 have day headers ("Today · 12 September", "Yesterday · 11 September") and a standing strip "You + Sam owe Alex + Jo €36.00", and frame 10 itemises "What makes up €84".
- Why it matters: there is no way to answer "what makes up the €84?" from the app. Tapping the Home balance opens this list filtered to a group, but the rows show expense totals (€96), not the share that creates the debt (€48), so the arithmetic cannot be followed.
- Fix: group by day with sticky headers ("Today", "Yesterday", "Thu 10 Sep", add the year when not current). Keep the summary strip pinned at the top for every filter. When opened from the balance, show the frame 10 treatment (items with "Your group's share of €96 · €48.00", a "Repayments recorded" line, a "Record a repayment" CTA); the full list is the other view. Running balance per row is not worth the noise on the main list. Put "Previously owed / Repayment / Still owed" (frames 37 and 39) on the repayment detail instead.
- Effort: M. Confidence: verified in code; design frames 10, 16 to 20.

### 10. Finding a past expense: amounts aren't searchable, filters are hidden, never reset, and group filter is clear-only
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: `AppModel.swift:5072-5088` searches title, category, note, recorder, group names and revision titles, but not amounts. Placeholder "Search merchant, person, or note" (`SheetViews.swift:72`) promises merchant and note, yet `ExpenseContent` has neither (`NumaSharedCore/Sources/NumaSharedCore/RecordContent.swift:23-32`; `note = nil` at `LedgerRepository.swift:1602`). `localizedCaseInsensitiveContains` is diacritic-sensitive, so "cafe" misses "café" (inferred). Kind and period live in two `Menu`s (`SheetViews.swift:93-117`), so state is only visible as a label inside a closed menu. `closeSheet()` and `openActivity()` reset only `activityFilter` (`AppModel.swift:4181-4189`); `activitySearchQuery`, `activityKindFilter` and `activityPeriodFilter` persist (`:329-331`). "See all" can therefore open on a stale "Expenses / This week / 'rent'" view. The group filter can be cleared but not chosen inside Activity (`SheetViews.swift:119-132`; it is only set by tapping a Home chip). The empty state says "No matching records / Try a different search or filter." even for a brand-new household (`:156-164`).
- Why it matters: "the €96 dinner" is how people remember things; and a stale hidden filter makes history look like it lost records.
- Fix: search amounts (match "96", "96.00", "€96") and use `.diacriticInsensitive`. Replace the two menus with visible chips: All / Expenses / Repayments / Changes, plus one "Filter" button for group and period with a count badge. Reset search, kind and period on every fresh open (keep them only across the Activity to detail to Activity round trip). Show "No records yet" when the ledger is empty and "No matches" with a "Clear filters" button when filtered. Change the placeholder to "Search title, person or amount".
- Effort: M. Confidence: verified in code (diacritics inferred).

### 12. Edit and void are only reachable by scrolling to three stacked buttons; the void reason is mandatory with no explanation
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: no `swipeActions`, `contextMenu` or `accessibilityAction` exists anywhere (grep over `*.swift`). The Activity list is a `ScrollView` plus `VStack`, not a `List`, so swipe actions are not possible as built. In detail, "Repeat expense", "Edit expense" and "Void record" are full-width capsules at the bottom after a `Spacer` (`SheetViews.swift:367-411`), so on long details they scroll below the fold, and the destructive one sits 16pt under Edit. Frame 12 puts a pencil in the nav bar and a quiet text "Void this expense". Void's "Confirm void" is disabled at 45% opacity until a reason is typed (`:787-794`) with no text saying it is required. The reducer does require a non-blank void reason (`Reducer.swift:285-286`, `DECISIONS.md:136`) while frames 14 and 15 mark the reason "optional", so design and contract disagree. The impact box on the void sheet prints a heading with nothing under it when balances don't change, for example a personal expense (`:770-781`, versus the edit sheet's "Balances stay the same." `:714-718`).
- Why it matters: the reducer requirement and the design conflict, and the app resolved it by blocking the primary button silently. Voiding a duplicate should be a ten-second action.
- Fix (Challenges a decision: "reason optional" in the design): keep the reason required, but make it one tap: chips "Added twice", "Wrong amount", "Wrong person", "Other…" (Other focuses the field). Move Edit to a nav-bar pencil and Repeat/Void to a "⋯" menu, and add a context menu on rows (long-press: Edit, Repeat, Void). If the list moves to `List` (see Ground-up C), add a trailing swipe "Void" and leading "Edit". Each has the tap path. Add helper text under a disabled CTA. Show "No effect on balances" in the void sheet's empty state. Add `accessibilityAction`s for the same commands.
- Effort: M. Confidence: verified in code.

### 13. Edit can't change who paid, who it was for or when, silently re-weights splits, and the impact copy is ledger-speak
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: `appendExpenseRevision` takes only title, amount, category (`LedgerRepository.swift:710-772`); payer and allocations are copied from the previous content. When the amount changes, allocations are re-weighted by their previous proportions with a floor of 1 (`:736-754`), so an exact split of €60/€36 becomes 56.25/33.75 style shares without saying so. The edit sheet's impact box prints before and after for every affected group (`SheetViews.swift:719-727`), for example "Alex + Jo gets back €84.00 → Alex + Jo gets back €81.00" plus the mirrored line for the other group. Revisions carry no reason (`EventPayloadStructs.swift:21-31`), so frame 14's "Reason · optional: The tip was counted twice." cannot exist without a schema addition.
- Why it matters: the most common correction is a wrong payer or split, and today the only fix is void plus re-add with no hint that this is the route. Two mirrored lines of "gets back / owes" force a mental diff.
- Fix: one sentence per edit, from the viewer's side: "Your group's debt drops from €84 to €81" with new shares listed under it as in frame 14 ("You + Sam New equal share €45.00"). Add a footer "To change who paid or who it was for, void this and add it again" until full editing exists. Real fix: reuse the Add expense flow prefilled in "Correct" mode; `ExpenseRevisedPayload` already carries payer and allocations, so no schema change is needed for this part. The optional reason needs an owner decision (schema field).
- Effort: S (copy) to L (full edit). Confidence: verified in code.

### 14. Undo lane: right idea, but no screen-reader announcement, no visible clock, thin wording
- Severity: S2 friction
- Evidence: window is `UndoPolicy.window = 6` (`NumaSharedCore/.../Undo.swift:12`). `UndoBar` (`Components.swift:669-689`) has no countdown affordance. A grep for `AccessibilityNotification`, `UIAccessibility.post` and `announce` over `*.swift` returns nothing, so the toast is silent to VoiceOver. Only the "Undo" text is a button (`:678-684`) and the label has no object ("Undo"). The Home lane appears with no transition (`HomeView.swift:69-72`) while the sheet lane animates (`SheetViews.swift:30-39`). Starting a second action overwrites `undoEventID` (`AppModel.swift:4947-4955`) so the first can no longer be undone. Wording: "Settlement €50 recorded", "%@ voided" and "%@ updated" use stored titles (so finding 2's "Sam paid your household voided"), and nothing says what is left owed. After Undo the bar just disappears.
- Why it matters: VoiceOver users effectively cannot undo anything in 6 seconds. Sighted users get no sense of how long they have.
- Fix: post an `AccessibilityNotification.Announcement` when the bar appears ("Repayment recorded. Undo available for 6 seconds") and mark the bar as one element with a custom "Undo repayment" action. Add a thin 6-second progress line inside the bar (hidden for Reduce Motion, replaced by static text). Wording: "Repayment recorded · You + Sam still owe €34", "Dinner corrected · €96 → €90", "Dinner voided". After Undo show "Undone" for a beat with a light haptic. Animate the Home lane like the sheet lane. Challenges a decision (6 s): voids and repayments are rare enough that a 10 s hold for them costs nothing but a few seconds of delayed sync; the constant is a core policy so this is the owner's call.
- Effort: S for accessibility, progress and copy; M if the window changes. Confidence: verified in code.

### 16. Activity and detail are one sheet whose content is swapped by an enum
- Severity: S2 friction

### 17. Smaller inconsistencies between Home, Activity and the sheets
- Severity: S3 polish
- Evidence:
  - Home's nav label says "Activity, N records" using `liveRecords.count` (`HomeView.swift:511`, voided excluded) while Activity lists voided rows too.

## Ground-up rethink

**A. History as a running story ("Our running tab").** Activity becomes a day-grouped feed where every change is its own plain sentence, as the approved frames 19 and 20 intended. A correction reads "Sam changed Dinner €96 to €90 · your group −€3.00". A void reads "Sam voided Dinner · 'Added twice' · no longer counts". A repayment reads "You + Sam paid Alex + Jo back €50 · still owe €34". The original rows stay, tagged "corrected" or "voided", with the amount struck when voided. A segmented control (All / Expenses / Repayments / Changes) replaces the two menus, and the standing balance strip stays pinned at the top. The user sees the whole life of a number without opening anything.

**B. One "Fix this" door for every record.** Detail gets a single nav-bar pencil. It opens a short sheet with the same three plain options for expenses and repayments: "Change the details" (amount, title, and eventually payer and split in the Add flow prefilled), "It shouldn't count" (void for expenses, reverse for repayments, one reason chip, one confirm), and "Record it again" (repeat). A one-line preview states the effect in words ("Your group's debt drops from €84 to €81"). Flag, reversal and revision are internal; the contract is untouched because the app still appends the same linked events, with a single Undo for the batch.

**C. A half-height repayment sheet where the common case needs no keypad.** Home's "Record a repayment" opens a `.medium` sheet: the two groups, a big "€84.00", chips "Full / Half / Other", and one CTA "Record €84.00 repaid". "Other" or tapping the amount brings up the keypad with replace-on-first-key. Overpayment shows the inline amber note and requires a second tap. The sheet speaks from the viewer's side ("You + Sam sent Alex + Jo" or "Alex + Jo received from You + Sam") because creditors record receipts as often as debtors record payments. After saving, the toast states what is left, and the repayment's detail has the "Previously owed / Repayment / Still owed" receipt from frames 37 and 39.

**D. A "My share" lens on history.** A switch at the top of Activity flips every row from the expense total (€96) to the effect on the viewer's group (−€48 or +€48), with the running balance as a quiet right-hand column. This answers "what makes up my €84?" and "who changed my balance?" without cluttering the default list, and it doubles as the frame 10 "Why this balance" screen: tapping the Home balance opens Activity in My share mode.
