# First-run UX and functional QA — 2026-07-18

Scope: a new App Store user on iPhone 17 Pro Max, from first launch through
creating a pot, adding people, recording and correcting an expense, reviewing
balances, recording a settlement, and checking Activity. The audit used the
native app, current seeded fixtures, XCUITest interactions, and screenshots
captured from the tested build.

## Changes made after the first pass

1. The home header no longer implies a pot switcher when only one pot is
   available, and its spending copy no longer implies an unavailable analytics
   period selector.
2. People shown after onboarding now come from the saved household roster;
   phantom sample people are no longer injected into management screens.
3. Add expense now supports a real merchant, category, payer, complete people
   picker, shares, and exact minor-unit allocations with sum validation.
4. Manual settlement now records any valid payer and recipient pair and shows
   the balance impact before persistence.
5. The out-of-scope analytics sheet and its dead switch path were removed.
6. Secondary text contrast and first-run helper copy were strengthened without
   changing the calm, identity-led color grammar.
7. The settlement plan uses a compact detent for short plans, removing the
   empty lower half that made the sheet look unfinished.
8. All five App Store screenshots were recaptured from the tested native flow,
   without debug controls or fabricated product states.

## Evidence and limits

- Automated coverage includes model/repository rules, the full native unit and
  UI suite, Release marker scanning, and two-simulator sync verification.
- Screenshots were inspected at 1320 × 2868 and are stored under
  `app-store/screenshots/en-US/6.9-inch/`.
- Simulator QA cannot prove physical-device haptic feel, real VoiceOver speech
  cadence, App Store processing, or production Convex/Apple identity behavior.
- Production URLs, support/legal values, and final App Store submission remain
  operator-controlled release inputs; they are not product UI regressions.
