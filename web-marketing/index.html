01 — The problem

Spreadsheets go quiet.
Relationships do not.

Shared rent, groceries, trips — the numbers hide in chats and half-updated sheets. Awkward reminders follow. Numa Shared turns the ledger into something you can read at a glance and update with one deliberate gesture.

04 — Settle

Fewest transfers. Explicit records.

Numa Shared proposes a settle plan. You record repayments after they happen outside the app — firm-hold a disc on the field, or use the quiet list. Partial, exact, or overpay with preview. Settlements stay immutable; corrections append openly.
